# Start a FAST market screen (not a full study)

Use `head-of-market-research` to conduct a FAST, INITIAL MARKET SCREEN of the project in `inputs/PROJECT_BRIEF.md`.
Engage `real-estate-hospitality` for demand and competitors, `investment-banking` for economic logic and red flags,
and `syndications` for initial financing blockers; engage the other specialists only if needed. Label the output
"MARKET SCAN — NOT A FULL FEASIBILITY STUDY". Log every run in `research/00_director/RUN_LOG.md`.

Return: key market evidence (with sources), five fatal assumptions to validate, competitor map, viability hypothesis,
research priorities, and a preliminary verdict (normally DEFER pending full study, or an indicative direction).
